RAAHI is a **community-driven travel platform** designed to help users explore **hidden gems across India**—places that are often overlooked by mainstream travel apps.

Instead of promoting crowded tourist destinations, RAAHI focuses on **authentic local experiences**, powered by contributions from locals, travelers, and guides.

---

##  Overview  

RAAHI combines **interactive maps, AI-powered recommendations, and a strong community ecosystem** to transform how people travel.

It bridges the gap between:
- Travelers seeking unique experiences  
- Locals with real knowledge  
- Small businesses needing visibility  

---

##  Key Features  

###  Hidden Gem Discovery  
- Explore underrated places using an **interactive map with custom pins**  
- Filter by categories like food, culture, markets, and more  
- View detailed place pages with photos, stories, and reviews  

---

###  AI-Powered Recommendations (Gem Genie)  
- Get suggestions using **natural language queries**  
- Discover places based on your vibe and preferences  

---

###  Community & Travel Buddy System  
- Connect with like-minded travelers  
- Find travel companions  
- Share experiences and reviews  

---

###  Local Guides & Booking  
- Browse and book **verified local guides**  
- Access authentic, guided experiences  
- Secure booking with integrated payments  

---

### Gamification  
- Earn **badges, points, and rewards**  
- Build your travel profile and credibility

##  How It Works  

1. User signs up and creates a profile  
2. Sets location or searches a city  
3. Explores hidden gems on the map  
4. Uses filters or AI for recommendations  
5. Connects with travel buddies or selects a guide  
6. Books and experiences the trip  
7. Shares reviews or adds new hidden gems
